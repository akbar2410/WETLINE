About this prototype

WETLINE turns peat water-table readings into a public rule that releases pre-approved rewetting and patrol money from Indonesia's Disaster Pooling Fund before fires start. This page demonstrates the four modules described in the essay. All station series, burned polygons and plan amounts on this page are generated for demonstration and are not field records. Thresholds default to values from Taufik et al. (2022) and PP 57/2016.
